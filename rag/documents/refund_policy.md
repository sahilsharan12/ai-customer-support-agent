
# Refund Policy

Policy Type: Refund Policy
Policy Version: 1.0
Policy Status: Demo Policy

## 1. Refund Eligibility

- Customers may request a refund for eligible returned orders.
- The order must be verified using a valid order ID.
- The return request must be approved before the refund process begins.
- Refund eligibility depends on the applicable return policy.
- Refunds are not automatically approved just because a customer requests them.

## 2. Refund Conditions

A customer may be eligible for a refund under the following circumstances:

- The returned product has been received and inspected.
- The product was damaged or defective.
- The customer received an incorrect product.
- The return request was approved by the support team.
- The order meets the applicable return conditions.

## 3. Refund Processing

- Refund processing starts only after the return is approved.
- Returned products may require inspection before refund approval.
- The support team must verify the refund amount.
- The refund must be processed through the authorized payment system.
- The customer should receive confirmation once the refund is successfully processed.

## 4. Refund Payment Methods

- Refunds should generally be issued through the original payment method.
- If the original payment method cannot receive the refund, human support must review the case.
- The AI assistant must not promise a specific payment processing time unless an approved policy or verified transaction record provides it.

## 5. Partial Refunds

- Partial refunds may apply when only some products from an order are eligible.
- Shipping charges may be treated differently depending on the reason for return.
- The final refundable amount must be confirmed by the support team.

## 6. Refund Status

Possible refund statuses:

- Not Requested
- Requested
- Under Review
- Approved
- Processing
- Completed
- Rejected

The AI assistant must distinguish between these statuses.

## 7. Refund Restrictions

- A refund request does not mean that a refund has been approved.
- An approved refund does not mean that the payment has been completed.
- The AI assistant must never claim that money has been refunded without transaction confirmation.
- The AI assistant must never invent a refund transaction ID.
- If refund information is unavailable, escalate the request to human support.

## 8. Human Approval

The following actions require human authorization:

- Approving or rejecting a refund.
- Determining the final refundable amount.
- Approving exceptions to the refund policy.
- Handling disputes about refund transactions.

The AI assistant can explain the policy and collect relevant information, but cannot independently authorize or process refunds.
