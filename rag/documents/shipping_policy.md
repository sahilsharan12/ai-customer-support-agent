
# Shipping and Delivery Policy

Policy Type: Shipping Policy
Policy Version: 1.0
Policy Status: Demo Policy

## 1. Order Processing

- Orders are processed after successful order confirmation.
- Order processing time may vary depending on product availability.
- Customers should receive an order confirmation after successful placement.
- Processing does not mean the order has been shipped.

## 2. Shipping and Dispatch

- Orders are dispatched after processing is completed.
- Customers may receive shipping confirmation once the order is dispatched.
- Shipping status must be verified using an authorized order tracking system.
- The AI assistant must not invent tracking information.

## 3. Estimated Delivery

- Estimated delivery dates are provided as approximate delivery timelines.
- Actual delivery dates may differ from estimated delivery dates.
- Delivery timelines may vary depending on location, logistics and external circumstances.
- The AI assistant must clearly distinguish estimated delivery from actual delivery.

## 4. Delayed Deliveries

If an order is delayed:

1. Verify the order ID.
2. Retrieve the available order information.
3. Check the estimated delivery date.
4. Explain the available information to the customer.
5. Escalate unresolved delivery issues to human support.

The AI assistant must not promise a new delivery date without verified information.

## 5. Failed Delivery

- If delivery is unsuccessful, the customer should contact support.
- The support team must verify the reason for failed delivery.
- Further delivery attempts depend on the logistics provider.
- The AI assistant must not claim that another delivery attempt has been scheduled without confirmation.

## 6. Missing or Damaged Deliveries

- Customers should report missing or damaged deliveries to support.
- Customers may be asked to provide photographs or supporting evidence.
- The support team must investigate the issue.
- Eligible cases may be reviewed under the return and refund policies.

## 7. Order Status Definitions

Common order statuses include:

- Created: Order record has been created.
- Approved: Order has been approved.
- Processing: Order is being prepared.
- Invoiced: Invoice has been generated.
- Shipped: Order has been dispatched.
- Delivered: Order has been marked as delivered.
- Canceled: Order has been canceled.
- Unavailable: Order is unavailable in the dataset.

## 8. Important Restrictions

- Historical order records must not be presented as live tracking information.
- Estimated delivery dates must not be presented as confirmed delivery dates.
- The AI assistant must never invent shipment tracking numbers.
- Unverified delivery claims must be escalated to human support.
